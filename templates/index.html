Rendering spectrogram…

    
    
Time runs left to right, frequency bottom to top, brightness is energy.
      A lossy ancestor shows as a dead band with a razor-straight edge across the top.
      A genuine master fades out unevenly and keeps noise all the way to the top of the picture.
      An old or deliberately dull master can look band-limited too — that is why a low confidence
      is worth a look before you delete anything.
